I'm Manas Ranjan Sahu, an AI and Flutter developer who likes turning machine-learning ideas into apps people can actually use. My work sits between deep learning and mobile engineering: I build and train models, then wrap them in clean, practical interfaces.

I'm pursuing an MCA at Sambalpur University Institute of Information Technology, after graduating B.Sc. Computer Science as a Gold Medalist (CGPA 8.8/10). I've built ReliefLink, an offline AI disaster-response system powered by a local Gemma model; WorkForge, a Flutter and Node.js app for contractor management; and a deep-learning landslide detection system for video. My approach is simple: understand the problem, prototype fast, and build for real-world conditions, like no internet.

Right now I'm exploring agentic AI, local LLMs and computer vision, and I'm open to opportunities as a Software Developer or AI Engineer.
